# Day 49–50: Partial start kyun mehnga hai

8-worker job. Cluster pe 4 GPUs khali hain. Bina gang ke 4 pods Running ho jaate hain aur AllReduce ka wait karte hain. Woh 4 GPUs doosri job nahi le sakti. Job khud aage nahi badhti.

Gang ho to 4 GPUs pe **0** pods chalte hain. GPUs doosri chhoti job le sakti hain. Jab 8 milen, yeh job ek saath start hoti hai.

Yeh Week 6 ke Slurm wale native gang ka Kubernetes version hai.

**Rule:** 7 of 8 running pods progress nahi hain. Woh held GPUs hain.
